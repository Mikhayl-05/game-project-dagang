How to Barter

1
Inspect the Three Merchants

Click any booth in the harbor marketplace (Fisherman, Woodcutter, or Blacksmith) to see what items they request and offer.

2
Plan Your 3 Turns

You begin with Salt x2 and 3 turns. Every trade uses 1 turn and reduces that merchant's stock.

3
Chain Trades to the Iron Sword

Trade goods step-by-step until you hold both materials required by the Blacksmith, then forge the Iron Sword!
